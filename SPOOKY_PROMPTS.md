## 🔮 Tips for Best Results

### Style Keywords
- **Vintage**: retro, classic, old-school, 1950s
- **Modern**: minimalist, clean, contemporary
- **Detailed**: intricate, elaborate, complex
- **Simple**: minimalist, clean lines, silhouette
- **Artistic**: watercolor, ink drawing, sketch

### Atmosphere Keywords
- **Dark**: shadowy, noir, black background
- **Eerie**: unsettling, creepy, disturbing
- **Mystical**: magical, enchanted, supernatural
- **Gothic**: Victorian, dark romantic, ornate
- **Horror**: terrifying, frightening, scary

### Color Suggestions
- Monochrome (black and white)
- Orange and black (classic Halloween)
- Purple and green (mystical)
- Red and black (horror)
- Blue and silver (ghostly)

### Composition Tips
- Add "centered composition" for balanced designs
- Use "silhouette" for bold, printable designs
- Include "high contrast" for better printing
- Mention "transparent background" if needed
- Add "vector style" for clean lines
