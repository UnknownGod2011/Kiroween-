## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
- **Background**: Pure black (#000000)
- **Primary**: Neon orange (#ff6b00)
- **Secondary**: Neon purple (#a259ff)
- **Accent**: Ghost white (#cdd6f4)
- **Danger**: Dark red (#8B0000)

### Typography
- **Hero Title**: 6xl-8xl, gradient text
- **Section Title**: 5xl, gradient text
- **Body**: Purple-300
- **Labels**: Orange-400

### Effects
- **Glow**: `shadow-[0_0_40px_rgba(255,107,0,0.6)]`
- **Blur**: `backdrop-blur-sm`
- **Gradient**: `from-orange-500 to-purple-500`
